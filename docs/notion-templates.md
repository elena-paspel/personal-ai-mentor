# Notion templates

Two Notion templates offer the same support as the companion page for learners who prefer to work in Notion. Both carry mentor instructions version 0.7.

## My AI Mentor (for learners)

A mentee-facing template laid out like a small website.

- **Home:** three "Start here" cards, the learner's sessions as a gallery of cards, a section on taking a question to a person, and short answers to common questions.
- **Start a session:** the mentor instructions in one box with a copy button.
- **How a session works:** the five stages in plain language, each with the label the mentor uses.
- **Talk to a person:** a checklist of signs that a question needs people, and a hand-over to fill in.

A learner presses Duplicate to get a private copy in their own Notion account, then keeps one card per session.

## AI Mentor Companion (reference layout)

A plainer layout with the stage table, command words and a sessions table whose columns hold the three continuity elements: central question, surfaced assumption and planned experiment.

## How the Notion templates differ from the companion page

| | Companion page | Notion template |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where the trace is stored | Only in the learner's browser | In the learner's Notion account, on Notion's servers |
| Account needed | None | A Notion account |
| Bringing a trace in | Sorted into the twelve lines automatically | Pasted into one box |
| Hand-over for a person | Built automatically from the trace | Filled in by hand, or drafted by the mentor |
| Editing the template | Requires changing the page's code | Done directly in Notion |

For a study with participants, the place where traces are stored is a data protection question and should be settled with the responsible institution.
