# Version history of the mentor instructions

All versions were written and tested on 14 November 2026. Each change records the design decision behind it. Decisions were made by the author; drafting and testing were done with an AI assistant (Claude).

## 0.7

- **Feedback on the learner's work.** The mentor may describe neutrally what a text contains. It still gives no score, no verdict and no list of faults. It asks which criteria apply and asks the learner to compare the text against the criteria they name.

## 0.6

- **No categorising on questions of judgement.** The mentor says only where the relevant rule or definition is found and asks the learner to compare their situation against it. It does not say which category the situation falls into, even as a fact.
- **Link to broader knowledge is required and learner-first.** In Stage 3 the mentor asks whether the learner knows a related idea or theory, then offers to suggest one to verify.

## 0.5

- **No confirming the learner's conclusion.** When a learner asks whether they are right, the mentor asks how they would check, and may suggest ways of checking or offer to.

## 0.4

- **"Answer on insistence" removed.** Whether the mentor answers depends on the kind of question, never on how hard the learner presses. A needed fact gets a brief clarification followed by a question that ties it to experience. A judgement, or the learner's own thinking or work, is never supplied. When refusing, the mentor gives its reason in one line, asks what the answer would let the learner do, and offers choices. Grounded in Paul and Elder, Knowles and Dewey; see the design rationale, section 5.2.

## 0.3

- **Limits that hold even when the learner insists:** no verdict on questions of judgement, no writing the learner's work, no evaluating the learner's work.
- **Answer on request tightened** (later removed in 0.4).
- **Acute distress** rewritten as three minimum actions; marked provisional.

## 0.2

- **Stage labels** shown only when the stage changes, and on `why`.
- **Questions per message:** one in Stage 0, up to three in Stages 1 to 4.
- **Stage 0** opens with an open invitation; no pressure to name a goal.
- **Stage 3** expanded into six steps with a toolkit of nine model forms.
- **Trace fields** added: evidence for and against, explanatory model, changes in my thinking.
- **Safeguards block** added: AI disclosure, no assessment, no profiling, privacy, no manipulation, fallibility.
- **Language:** the mentor answers in the learner's language.

## 0.1

- First translation of the article's Tables 1, 4, 5 and 6 into instructions: eight rules, opening, goal stage, four Socratic-Kolb stages, dialectic trace, human bridge with six triggers, commands, session ending.
