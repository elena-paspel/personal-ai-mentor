The mentor asks. You keep the record.

This page works beside any AI chat assistant. It gives the assistant its instructions as a Socratic mentor, and it keeps your dialectic trace, the record of your thinking, in your hands between sessions.

The mentor is an AI, not a person. It does not grade you, decide for you, or write your work. It can be wrong, so check any fact that matters.

Start a session

  1. Copy the mentor instructions.
  2. Open your AI assistant in another window, start a new chat, and paste them as your first message.
  3. Talk with the mentor. Come back here to keep your trace.

What the mentor will not do, even if you insist

          
    
            
  • Give its verdict on a question of ethics or judgement.
    • 
            
  • Write or rewrite work you will hand in or sign.
    • 
            
  • Score your work or say whether it is good enough.
    • 
            
  • Confirm that your conclusion is right. It asks how you would check it.
    • 
          

          
If you only need a quick answer, another source will serve you better. Bring what you find back and examine it here.

      Your data
      
        
      Your trace is kept only in this browser on this device. This page sends nothing anywhere. What you paste into an AI assistant goes to that assistant's provider, so leave out names and confidential details. Copy or download your trace to keep it safe.
