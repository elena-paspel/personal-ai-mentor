Start a session See how it works

Free. Works with the AI chat assistant you already use. No account, and nothing you write here leaves your browser.

Why use a mentor that asks?

When an AI hands you an answer, the thinking is its own, and you still have to stand behind the result. This mentor keeps the thinking with you.

How this mentor differs from a usual AI chat
A usual AI chatThis mentor
Gives you an answer straight awayAsks questions that help you work out your own answer, and gives short facts when you need them
Will often write the work you hand inNever writes work you will submit or sign, so it stays yours
Tends to agree with what you already thinkAsks how you would check it
Gives its opinion on what you should doWon't judge for you. It shows where the rules are and helps you prepare to raise it with a person
Forgets you when the chat endsGives you notes to save and bring back, so the next session picks up where you stopped

How it works

This page gives your AI assistant a set of instructions that turn it into the mentor. You talk to the mentor there, and keep your notes here.

  1. Copy the instructions

    One click on this page.

  2. Paste them into a new chat

    In the AI assistant you already use. It replies as your mentor and asks what is on your mind.

  3. Keep your notes

    The mentor builds a record of your thinking as you talk. Save it here and paste it back next time.

Some questions need people

            
The mentor tells you when a question goes beyond what an AI should settle, and you can decide that yourself. This builds a short note to start that conversation, from your own notes.

      About this mentor
      
      
      
        
      It is an AI, not a person
      It doesn't grade you, decide for you or write your work. It can be wrong, so check any fact that matters.
      
        
      Your notes stay with you
      This page sends nothing anywhere. Your notes are kept only in this browser. Copy or download them to keep them safe.
